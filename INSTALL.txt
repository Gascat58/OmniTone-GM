OmniTone GM - installing
========================

1. Close Mixcraft.

2. Copy the whole "OmniTone GM.vst3" folder into:
       C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
   (Windows will ask for permission - click Continue.)

3. Get a General MIDI SoundFont (.sf2) if you don't have one.
   A good free one is GeneralUser GS. Put it somewhere permanent,
   for example  C:\SoundFonts\

4. Start Mixcraft. Go to Preferences > Plug-Ins and click
   "Re-Scan All VST Plug-Ins".

5. In Preferences > Plug-Ins, set
       Default General MIDI (GM) Instrument  =  OmniTone GM

6. Add OmniTone GM to any track once, open its window, click
   "Browse..." and pick your main SoundFont. This is saved for
   every OmniTone track from now on.

Choosing sounds
---------------
- Each imported track picks its sound from Controller 3 (CC 3),
  which should hold the same number as the track's Patch event.
  Mixcraft removes Patch events on import, but keeps CC 3.
- No CC 3 on a track = it plays piano (drums on channel 10 always work).
- The bottom of the OmniTone window shows what the track is playing.

Choosing this track's sound
---------------------------
Click any instrument in the list and it becomes this track's sound
(Mixcraft's preset dropdown at the top works too).

Replacing an instrument
-----------------------
Select a row, click "Replace...", pick any .sf2 file, then pick the
sound inside it. "Reset to main" puts it back. Double-click a row
(or click Preview) to hear it.

Backing up and sharing a sound set
----------------------------------
"Export Set..." packs your settings AND every SoundFont they use into
one .otgm file - a complete backup, or something to give a friend.
"Import Set..." loads one. Your current set is backed up first (as
soundset-before-import-<date>.json next to the settings file).
Imported SoundFonts go in  %APPDATA%\OmniTone GM\Sound Sets\
Only share SoundFonts whose licence allows it.

Your settings are stored in:
    %APPDATA%\OmniTone GM\soundset.json
